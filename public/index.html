We use a high-efficiency Greedy Cash Flow Minimization Algorithm (powered by a Java-based debt resolution engine). It calculates each member's net balance and greedily matches the largest debtors with the largest creditors, resolving complex multi-person group debts in the minimum possible direct transfers.

Suppose 3 friends have group expenses: Sarthak owes Hrudayesh ₹500, and Hrudayesh owes Harsh ₹500.

Without simplification, that requires 2 separate payments. Our greedy cash flow algorithm removes the middle person (Hrudayesh) and reduces it to 1 direct transfer: Sarthak pays Harsh ₹500 directly.

In large group dinners and road trips with complex shared debts, this eliminates over 70% of unnecessary transfers and settles everyone with zero confusion.

Each friend's exact debt is encoded into a custom UPI QR code. Friends scan with GPay, PhonePe, or Paytm to pay directly to your VPA.
